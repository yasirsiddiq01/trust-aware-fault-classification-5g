# GATE-5G

GATE-5G is a governance and assurance layer for AI-assisted 5G fault classification.

It controls whether a model output may be exposed as a diagnosis, used to make a recommendation, escalated for human approval, rejected through abstention, or blocked because mandatory evidence or policy requirements failed.

## Current scope

- Retrospective governance evaluation using frozen Study 1 evidence.
- Partial governance integration for the independent Study 2 approach.
- Deterministic policy enforcement.
- Human-controlled operational authority.
- Evidence provenance and study isolation.
- Tamper-evident audit records.

GATE-5G does not modify the published Study 1 evidence and does not mix Study 1 and Study 2 records.
