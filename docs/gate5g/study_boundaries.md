# Study Boundaries

## Study 1

Study 1 is frozen published evidence. GATE-5G may read its records and model outputs for retrospective governance evaluation.

GATE-5G must not modify, relabel, replace, or represent governance as part of the original Study 1 experiment.

## Study 2

Study 2 uses a completely different experimental approach, a separately constructed setup, and genuinely new records.

Its current CSV and evidence package are incomplete. Existing Study 2 records remain independent and must not be supplemented with Study 1 data.

## Relationship

The relationship between the studies is research lineage only:

1. The Study 1 research paper and findings motivated Study 2.
2. Study 2 built its own experimental setup and generated its own records.
3. No Study 1 record is part of Study 2.

## Prohibited operations

- Merging Study 1 and Study 2 records
- Reconstructing Study 2 from Study 1 evidence
- Study 2 fallback to Study 1 data
- Reusing Study 1 calibration thresholds for Study 2
- Presenting mixed-study batches as valid evidence
